I am an incoming assistant professor, starting Fall 2027, in the Chemical and Biomolecular Engineering ([ChemBE](https://engineering.jhu.edu/chembe/)) department at Johns Hopkins University, where I will also be affiliated with the Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Institute ([DSAI](https://ai.jhu.edu)). My lab's [research](/research/) will center on developing AI-driven workflows to enable autonomous biomolecular engineering. I am actively recruiting graduate students and postdocs -- please see [this page](/team/) for more information!

I am currently a [Tayebati Postdoctoral Fellow](https://computing.mit.edu/research/postdoctoral-fellows-programs/tayebati-postdoctoral-fellowship-program/) at MIT, coadvised by [Professor Heather Kulik](https://hjkgrp.mit.edu) and [Professor Tommi Jaakkola](https://people.csail.mit.edu/tommi/). Before, I completed my PhD in chemical engineering at Caltech, coadvised by [Professor Frances Arnold](http://fhalab.caltech.edu) and [Professor Yisong Yue](http://www.yisongyue.com). During my doctoral studies, I also interned at [Profluent](https://www.profluent.bio), and I was a co-organizer for the ML for protein engineering [seminar series](https://www.ml4proteinengineering.com).

Outside of research, I enjoy [electronic music](https://open.spotify.com/playlist/4RPKYnhJRLfIiuhpknQyNw?si=7f38795ed03c4194), exploring new places and foods, and learning about history and philosophy. I also enjoy teaching, mentoring, and collaboration. Please reach out!
