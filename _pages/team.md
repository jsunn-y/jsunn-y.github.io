## Join Us!

We are actively recruiting motivated students and postdocs to join the lab in September 2027.

### Prospective MS and PhD Students

We are looking for students who are excited to work on foundational questions in AI for protein engineering. Students with experience in machine learning, molecular modeling, protein engineering, or biochemistry are especially encouraged to apply.

I am primarily involved with admissions through the [ChemBE program](https://engineering.jhu.edu/chembe/academics/graduate-studies/phd-program/) at JHU. The program admits as a cohort, so please apply through there and mark down your interest in working with me in the application. The deadline for the Fall 2027 PhD program is **December 1, 2026**. Applicants that would fit better with other programs, such as Computational Biology and Computer Science, can feel free to [email me](mailto:jyang4@mit.edu) as well.   

### Prospective Postdoctoral Researchers

We welcome applicants with strong training in **experimental protein engineering/synthetic biology** and an interest in developing wet-lab enzyme engineering workflows that are integrated with machine learning methods. Candidates should have a Ph.D. in chemical engineering, biological engineering, biochemistry, chemistry, or related fields. Previous experience with machine learning, computational chemistry, and/or computational biology is helpful, but not required. 

If this sounds like a good fit, please [email me](mailto:jyang4@mit.edu) with your CV, a few sentences on your background and future reesearch interests in the lab, and contact information for 2 references. 

### Prospective Undergraduate Students
Undergraduate students of all backgrounds at Johns Hopkins can feel free to reach out if you are interested in getting involved with research starting in fall 2027!
