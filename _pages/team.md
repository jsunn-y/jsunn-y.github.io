## Team Philosophy

We are especially interested in scientists who are excited by interdisciplinary work and who enjoy building tools that connect large-scale computational reasoning with biology in the lab. We welcome students and postdocs with backgrounds in chemical engineering, computer science, statistics, applied math, chemistry, molecular biology, or related fields.

## Join Us!

We are actively recruiting graduate students and postdoctoral scholars to join the lab in Fall 2027.

### Prospective PhD Students

We are looking for students who are excited to work on foundational questions in AI for biology and protein engineering. Students with experience in machine learning, optimization, molecular modeling, biochemistry, or experimental protein engineering are encouraged to apply.

I am primarily involved with admissions through the [ChemBE program](https://engineering.jhu.edu/chembe/academics/graduate-studies/phd-program/) at JHU. The program admits as a cohort, so please apply through there and mark down your interest in working with me in the application. Applicants from other programs such as Computational Biology and Computer Science can feel free to email me as well.   

### Prospective Postdoctoral Scholars

We welcome applicants with strong research training in experimental protein engineering and/or synthetic biology and an interest in developing wet-lab workflows that are integrated with machine learning methods. Previous experience with machine learning, computational chemistry, or computational biology is helpful, but not required. 

If you are interested in joining, please email me with your CV, and a few sentence description of your background and future reesearch interests in the lab.
